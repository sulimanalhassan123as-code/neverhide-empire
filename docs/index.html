📖 How to Install & Use

  1. Download the APK above, tap it, and allow Install unknown apps for your browser when asked.
  2. Open the app and tap Grant Missing Permissions — allow everything (location, calls, SMS, camera, mic, calendar, sensors). The suite needs them for its tools.
  3. Tap Enable Lock Guardian and activate device admin — this powers the wrong-password jumpscare.
  4. Pick a live wallpaper (20 effects), set it, and enjoy. Explore the 16 Power Tools in the toolkit.
  5. Updates: the app checks automatically at every open — or tap Check for Updates any time.

🔒 Privacy

• No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Everything runs on your phone.

• Intruder selfies are stored only in the app's private folder on your device — never uploaded anywhere.

• The SIM Guard SMS goes only to the trusted number you set, from your own phone.

• The only network calls the app ever makes: weather lookup (Open-Meteo) and its own update check (GitHub).

🆘 Help & Contact

• Wrong password triggered a scare you didn't want? Tap the jumpscare to close it; disable Guardian in the app.

• Siren won't stop? Open the app → Find My Phone → Stop, or reboot.

• Contact the developer through the Neverhide Empire Telegram assistant (link shared with the app).
